We record every business named and every source cited, and publish all of it here, right or wrong. This is the July 2026 edition.

Why this exists

Business owners ask whether AI recommends them. Agencies guess at how AI picks sources. The global indexes that exist, Promvia's AI Source Index, BrandInsightAI's GEO Index and HeyAmos's AI Sources Index among them, answer at the level of "best CRM" and report domains worldwide. None of them asks about a roofer in DeLand or names the businesses. This one does, every month, on a fixed panel, so the record can be compared over time and reproduced by anyone with the prompt list and the method.

It began as EVOIX's Central Florida AI Citation Study, collected 2 July 2026. That run is month zero here and the panel has not changed since.

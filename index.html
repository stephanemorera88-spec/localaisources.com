About this book

Every month, on the first Monday, we ask 4 AI engines the same 100 questions, "Who is the best {trade} in {city}, Florida?", for 10 trades across 10 Central Florida cities, with live web search on. We record every business named and every source cited, and print all of it here, right or wrong. A business listed on a page was named by that engine on that date. It is not an EVOIX recommendation, and absence is not a judgement of any business.

The global indexes that exist, Promvia's AI Source Index, BrandInsightAI's GEO Index and HeyAmos's AI Sources Index among them, answer at the level of "best CRM" and report domains worldwide. None of them asks about a roofer in DeLand or names the businesses. This one does, on a fixed panel, so the record can be compared over time and reproduced by anyone with the prompt list and the method. It began as EVOIX's Central Florida AI Citation Study, collected 2 July 2026; that run is month zero here and the panel has not changed since. The full November 2026 run, with every engine's figures and most cited sources, is on the month page; the rows are free to download.
